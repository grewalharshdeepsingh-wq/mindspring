# 🌿 MindSpring — Personalised Student Wellness & Emotional Growth Platform

MindSpring is an evidence-based digital self-care and mental wellbeing platform designed to help students manage academic stress, build sustainable study habits, and develop emotional resilience without toxic competition or punitive streaks.

Rather than offering generic meditation advice, MindSpring uses a **deterministic cause-based matching matrix** that maps specific student stressors (such as exam overwhelm, procrastination, low confidence, peer comparison, family expectations, and sleep debt) into achievable, bite-sized **2–10 minute micro-growth activities**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🎯 60-Second Student Check-In Pipeline
- **Emotional Scan:** Select immediate state (Calm, Uplifted, Scattered, Anxious, Overwhelmed, Exhausted).
- **Cause-Based Diagnosis:** Choose primary root stressor (Exam & Revision Overwhelm, Task Avoidance & Procrastination, Fear of Failure, Social Comparison, Family Expectations, Sleep Debt, Social Friction).
- **Micro-Goal Target:** Pick or write a small, concrete victory for the day.

### 2. 💡 Empathetic Problem Solver & Daily Motivator
- **Brief Problem Understanding:** Students can type any dilemma in their own words or select common scenarios (exam backlog, procrastination paralysis, bad grades & parental expectations, peer exclusion, burnout). The intelligent engine breaks down the biological and cognitive root cause and validates the student's feelings without judgment.
- **Actionable 3-Tier Solution:**
  - ⚡ **Immediate 5-Minute Relief:** Friction-free triage action to calm the nervous system or break task freeze right now.
  - 🎯 **Step-by-Step Strategic Roadmap:** Phased tactical plan to realistically conquer the challenge.
  - 🛠️ **Direct Tool Integration:** Immediate launch button for recommended tools (Box Breathing, 5-Min Study Sprint, Thought Flip, etc.).
- **High-Impact Motivation & Pep Talk:**
  - Personalized, uplifting pep talk tailored to their exact issue.
  - **🌟 Golden Power Affirmation:** Memorable mindset anchor.
  - **🔊 Audio Narration (Web Speech API):** Listen to the encouraging words spoken aloud with play/pause controls.
  - **1-Click Actions:** Add the action item directly into the Daily Plan, save the breakthrough to the Growth Journal, copy the solution to clipboard, or revisit past solved problems in the History drawer.

### 3. 📋 Balanced 4-Part Daily Regimen
Every day is structured into 4 balanced, self-paced micro-tasks:
1. **Stress-Relief Activity:** (e.g., Box Breathing, 4-7-8 Parasympathetic breath, 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding, Progressive Muscle Relaxation)
2. **Growth Activity:** (e.g., BJ Fogg 5-Minute Study Start, CBT "Thought Flip" Reality Check, Self-Compassion letter)
3. **Practical Stressor Action:** (e.g., Syllabus Chunking Matrix, 2-Minute Desk Clear, Comparison Shield, Parent Dialogue Script, Nightly Sleep Checklist)
4. **Optional Fun & Mindful Break:** (e.g., Mindful Zen Doodle Canvas, Gratitude Garden, Brain Refresh Riddles, Ambient Soundscapes)

### 3. 📊 Data & Progress Tracking Table
- **Interactive Ledger:** Automatically logs completed check-ins and activities with dates, student name, school, stressor, mood shift, rating, and reflections.
- **Log New Entries:** Custom entry creation for self-directed study sessions.
- **Spreadsheet Export:** 1-Click **Export to CSV** (compatible with Excel & Google Sheets) and JSON export.
- **Search & Filter:** Instantly filter by stressor or search personal notes.

### 4. 🎮 Zen Arcade & Mini-Games Suite
- **👻 Ghostface Door Runner 🔪:** An energetic 60 FPS 3D-perspective corridor runner featuring Ghostface sprinting with a knife toward upcoming logic doors.
  - **Common Sense & Logic Questions:** Answer witty riddles displayed at the top of the screen (e.g., *"What gets wetter the more it dries?"*).
  - **Door Mechanics:** Choose between Door A and Door B (using keyboard `[A]/[D]`, arrow keys, touch, or on-screen buttons).
  - **Victory Dance vs. Tantrum Crying:**
    - Choose the **Correct Door**: Confetti explodes and Ghostface does a hilarious disco victory dance waving his knife in the air (`200 IQ MOVE! 🕺✨`).
    - Choose the **Wrong Door**: Ghostface drops his knife, lays face-down on the floor, kicks his legs tantrum-style, and sobs hysterically with shooting tear fountains while displaying the explanation (`WHYYYY?! 😭💦`).
  - **Funny Side Cameos:** Random characters (such as a cool Moonwalking Duck with sunglasses, Dancing Banana with maracas, and Chill Capybara) pop into the outer margins with funny speech bubbles without blocking gameplay.
- **🫧 Stress Bubble Pop:** Pop away exam worries & build combo streaks.
- **👽 Cosmic Alien Runner:** Claim donuts, muffins, and trigger 3-second Fart Boost flights!
- **🌸 Lotus Calm Catch:** Glide to catch glowing stars & dewdrops at 60 FPS.

### 5. 🧘 Interactive Wellness Studio
- **Guided Breathing Studio:** Visual expanding animated lung/sphere with custom modes (Box Breathing, 4-7-8, Physiological Sigh) and harmonic chimes.
- **BJ Fogg 5-Minute Study Start:** Circular animated countdown timer with ambient focus sounds and celebratory confetti.
- **CBT 3D "Thought Flip":** Interactive 3D flip card to identify cognitive traps (catastrophizing, all-or-nothing, mind reading) and flip them into objective, empowering reframes.
- **Mindful Zen Doodle Canvas:** HTML5 interactive drawing pad with calming pastel brushes, thickness control, and image download.
- **Gratitude Garden:** Digital garden where gratitude notes blossom into colorful animated flowers.

### 5. 🎧 Procedural Ambient Soundscapes (Web Audio API)
- Generated live in the browser without external streaming dependencies or buffering.
- Multi-track mixer for **Gentle Rain**, **Ocean Waves**, **Binaural Alpha Waves (10 Hz)**, and **Tibetan Meditation Bells**.

### 6. 🛡️ Ethical Safety Guardrails & Crisis Escalation
- Explicit Non-Diagnostic Medical Boundary Notice.
- Direct confidential school counselor message generator.
- 24/7 Verified Helplines: **Tele-MANAS** (`14416` / `1800-891-4416`), **Childline** (`1098`), **KIRAN** (`1800-599-0019`), and **Vandrevala Foundation**.

### 7. 🔒 Strict Privacy-by-Design Architecture
- **Self-Configurable Profile:** Student name and school are completely empty by default for individual personalization.
- **Zero Public Rankings:** No leaderboards, social feeds, or comparison metrics to guarantee psychological safety.
- **Non-Punitive Tracking:** Active mindful days never reset on missed days.
- **Local Data Protection:** Client-side encrypted reflections, one-click JSON/CSV data export, and instant data purge.
- **High Contrast & Dark Mode:** Accessibility-focused visual themes.
